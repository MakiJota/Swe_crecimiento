# Urban growth in Sweden's cities, 1990-2020

How the 35 largest Swedish cities (SCB tätorter, 2020) grew between 1990 and 2020, measured with the classes of the **Atlas of Urban Expansion** (Angel et al. 2016) on the **GISD30** impervious-surface record (Landsat, 30 m).

The question behind every page: **how much does it change the picture to count the fringe open space** (the 100 m ring of unbuilt land around urban and suburban built-up) as part of the city?

## In one paragraph

Across the 35 cities, built-up land inside the urban extent grew from 662 km² in 1990 to 1,051 km² in 2020 (1.55 % a year); without Stockholm, from 546 to 816 km² (1.35 % a year). The fringe open space is 41.7 % of the total urban extent in 2020, and between 29.5 % and 57.1 % city by city. Counting it adds 42-133 % to the area of each city, and moves its 1990-2020 growth rate by less than 0.2 points a year in 20 of the 35.
