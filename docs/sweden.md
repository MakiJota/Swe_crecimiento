## How much the fringe open space matters

### On the size of the city

In 2020 the fringe is between 29.5 % and 57.1 % of the urban extent; counting it adds 42-133 % to the area of everything else. Anything divided by the urban extent (population density, share of the city in a given condition) moves by that much depending on whether the fringe is in.

![Fringe share 1990 and 2020]({{ '/assets/img/en/sverige_franja.png' | relative_url }})

### On growth

Counting the fringe moves the 1990-2020 growth rate by less than 0.2 points a year in 20 of the 35 cities. It lowers growth most where the city fills in its old fringe ([Luleå]({{ '/cities/lulea/' | relative_url }}), [Södertälje]({{ '/cities/sodertalje/' | relative_url }}), [Karlstad]({{ '/cities/karlstad/' | relative_url }})) and raises it most where new scattered patches add new fringe ([Gävle]({{ '/cities/gavle/' | relative_url }}), [Östersund]({{ '/cities/ostersund/' | relative_url }}), [Borlänge]({{ '/cities/borlange/' | relative_url }})).
